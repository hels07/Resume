# Surface brief: index.html (portfolio)

Scope: the whole single-file portfolio. Visitor mode: **Experience** (the work leads from the first viewport).
Audience: admissions readers from the Common App link, then teachers and future contacts. Job: in three minutes, learn four concrete things Raees built and led, and reach him in one click.
Constraints: one HTML file; no SAT scores or grades; no list of target universities; the school-site repo is private; the forecaster is labelled as running on simulated data. ## Direction contract

THESIS: You have stepped into Dracula's great hall at midnight. Raees's name arches over a black marble floor that mirrors candle flames, under an iron chandelier hanging against a moonlit gothic window. Every project is a parchment card with an inset frame, checkerboarded against a violet-glass tile that carries a big white numeral. This refuses the category default: a light card grid with a headshot, skill bars and a timeline.

OWN-WORLD:
- The UI palette is plum #3b1f5a (deepest #120a1c), lavender #b9a6e0 and blood #c0283f (with #8c1426 for text on parchment).
- Parchment #ece2d0 cards carry a 1px inset frame, with ink #1c1418 on parchment and white on the scene.
- Gloock is set in uppercase for titles, numerals and the arched wordmark, with a hand-drawn crossbar-less A. Rokkitt slab serif carries body text and the numbered nav ("01/ WORK").
- There is one round seal with text on a circle; on parchment it is a blood-red wax seal.
- The 3D world is realistic and detailed, never rounded off:
  - pointed arcades, ribbed groin vaults and clustered piers, all in rough ashlar with normal-mapped relief
  - polished black marble with blurred real reflections
  - iron candelabra with flickering flames
  - leaded stained glass in deep jewel tones
  - moonlight through the great window, casting the tracery's shadow
  - violet haze, cobwebs, tattered crimson banners, bats and falling dark-rose petals

STORY: The reader meets Raees and Addis in one line. They open five numbered pieces of proof (the school website, a playable Connect 4 trainer, the Elo essay's data, the canteen forecaster, the home server). They read his one-sentence creed in giant type, see what he leads, then email him as the moon turns blood red.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed WebGL interior looks down the candlelit nave toward a grand stair and a tall gothic window with the moon behind it. An iron chandelier hangs in silhouette against the glass. "RAEES" arches huge in white over the marble, with "F E Y S A L  T E M A M" arched small above. The numbered nav sits top right with "Let's talk", and the logo star top left. The tagline and a parchment "Twelve years at Sandford" card sit upper left over a plum scrim. Four fanned parchment project cards sit at the right. The round seal turns at the lower right. A Connect 4 disc, a medal and a paper plane float in the nave.

FORM: The user pinned this direction (a gothic "Dracula's mansion" world in deep purple), keeping the THEMYSCIRA UI language (arched wordmark, seal, numbered nav, checkerboard plates, giant-type statement band) and the Collect UI portfolio layout. The pin beats the roll. Roll key 5b557a6f (assigned index 7), superseded by the pin.
RAISE (kept): numbers move with ballistic damping; solving a Connect 4 puzzle lights a candle on the chandelier that stays lit; the active section shows in the nav; the projects are a numbered "01 / 05" sequence.
Signature transition: as the reader scrolls out of the hero, it tilts back in 3D like a turning page. Meanwhile the camera walks down the nave, looks up into the vault under the chandelier, rises above the stair, and pulls back toward the doors as the moon turns blood red for the contact close.
